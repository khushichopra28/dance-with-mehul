01 / the story behind the dance

Meet The Founder,

I'm Mehul.

✦
⌇

For me, dance has always been about bringing people together — and there's no better place for that than a wedding. I've spent years choreographing sangeets, bride entries, and family performances where the goal isn't perfection, it's pure joy. Watching grandparents, cousins, and couples who've never danced before light up on the wedding floor — that's what I live for.

Every wedding is its own story, and I help you tell yours through movement. Whether it's a high-energy sangeet or an intimate couple's moment, I make sure every routine feels natural, heartfelt, and completely yours.

with love and rhythm,
Mehul
